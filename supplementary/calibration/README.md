> **SIMULATED RATERS. NOT HUMAN DATA AND NOT RESULTS OF THE STUDY.** The ratings come from `calibration/simulate.py`, whose generating process is known. Seed 7. Generated by `edufidelity calibrate --simulated --resamples 200`.
