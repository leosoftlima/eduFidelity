What can be composted

A healthy compost pile needs two kinds of material. Greens are moist and rich in nitrogen: fruit and vegetable scraps, coffee grounds and fresh grass clippings.

Browns are dry and rich in carbon: fallen leaves, shredded cardboard and small twigs. Aim for roughly three parts browns to one part greens.

Tip: if the pile smells sour, it has too many greens. Add a layer of dry leaves.
